REQUIREMENTS
These rules were checked and apply to this process.
- [GDPR-Art13-1] When the customer is contacted during the inquiry, they are told about the company's identity, contact details, and the purpose of data processing.
- [GDPR-Art13-2-a] When the customer is contacted during the inquiry, they are told how long their data will be kept, and the reasons if it's not possible to say.
- [GDPR-Art5-1-a] Personal data must be handled in a fair, clear, and lawful way when the customer is being asked about a product or service.
- [GDPR-Art5-1-e] The customer's details are kept only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected.
