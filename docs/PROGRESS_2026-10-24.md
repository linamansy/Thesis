### Small decisions (decided myself, with reasons)

- **Rule first:** section 5 of `docs/C3_V2_DEVELOPMENT.md` was committed (54cc675) before the first download.
- **ECD and OSH exist on CELLAR only as legacy HTML.** Asking for XHTML gives HTTP 404 ("does not hold a content
  datastream of the requested type"); Formex is not offered either; `Accept: text/html` gives the legacy EUR-Lex HTML
  (the only text manifestation). It was downloaded the same way otherwise and is kept byte-identical (`format:
  legacy_html` in `sources.json`).
- **Legacy HTML → chunker markup (new file `src/legacy_html_study2_laws.py`).** The old HTML is a flat list of `<p>`
  lines without article markup, so the unchanged chunker would find 0 articles. The converter only adds the XHTML
  structure (articles, titles, `NNN.MMM` paragraphs, point tables) in a temporary folder; `src/retrieval/chunker.py`
  was not touched, and the chunking rules are Study 1's. It removes nothing but chapter/section headings, footnote
  markers right after a word (ECD: 4, e.g. "Directive 89/48/EEC(27)"; a reference such as "Article 1(2)" is kept)
  and everything after "Done at" (signatures, footnotes, annex; the chunker drops annexes too). A word check proves
  no word of any article is lost (ECD and OSH: 0 lost words).
- **Joined lines in the legacy HTML:** a point label alone on a line ("(a)") is joined with its text; a line that
  starts in lower case continues the line before (the old HTML breaks some sentences, e.g. OSH Art 5(4)).
- **"(i)" after "(h)":** read as the letter i only if no "(ii)" follows in the same paragraph (OSH Art 6(2)(i) is a
  letter; ECD Art 2 (i)/(ii) are sub-points of (h)).
- **Untitled old articles:** OSH Art 4, 13 and 19 have no title in the official text; their heading ends in
  "Article 4: " (empty title, like the chunker does for any untitled article).
- **OSH entry into force:** the 1989 text has no entry-into-force article (it took effect on notification to the
  Member States, a date not in the text), so `entry_into_force.date` is null with that explanation; the
  application date is the Article 18(1) transposition deadline (31 Dec 1992).
- **Over-long passages:** CCD-Art2-2-h (1,657), CCD-Art2-6-a (1,984) and CCD-Art10-1 (1,564 characters) exceed
  `chunking.max_passage_chars` (1,500); kept by Study 1's existing rule (points are never cut; text after the last
  point stays with it; a paragraph without points is not split). Only CCD-Art10-1 is in a kept article.
- **Article decisions are a data file** (`data/knowledge_study2/article_selection.json`, every article with
  keep/drop and a reason); the chunk script refuses to run if an article is missing or its title differs.
- **Articles whose duties fall only on others** (Member States, Commission, intermediaries, individual workers)
  are dropped: they set no duty for the organisation, so the keep part of the rule does not cover them.
