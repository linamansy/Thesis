## Silver limitations

The GDPR development silver labels (`data/silver_gdpr/silver_reference_dev.json`, gpt-oss-120b via Groq, Study 1's
prompt unchanged) are AI-made and provisional. Three of the four applicable labels are doubtful; the prompt is kept
(Lina, 24 Oct 2026), and this is reported as a limitation:

1. **GDPR-Art2-1, case 11 (rerank), material scope:** labelled applicable with no required steps. Article 2(1) says
   when the GDPR applies; it is a scope article, not an obligation, so "applicable" adds no process step.
2. **GDPR-Art32-2, case 11 (rerank), security of processing:** labelled applicable, although the annotator itself lists
   two facts as not_stated ("Controller assesses security risks", "Appropriate security measures are implemented").
   Both are obligations of the article, not conditions; they are also its two required steps.
3. **GDPR-Art13-2-a, case 45 (hybrid only), information at collection:** the same pattern: applicable with one
   obligation listed as a not_stated fact ("Information must be provided at the time personal data are obtained").
   Not in the rerank top 5, so it does not affect the Study 2 scores.

This is Problem 2 of `docs/C3_V2_DEVELOPMENT.md` section 1 (obligations treated as conditions), now seen in the
silver annotator. It does not change the selection result: without GDPR-Art2-1, rerank still wins (2 vs 1).
