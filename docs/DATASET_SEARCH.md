# Search for better data than PMo (16 Oct 2026)

**Question:** is there a public dataset that pairs process descriptions with a BPMN reference **and** with the legal or regulatory requirements that apply to each process?
**Answer:** I found none. That supports your research gap; phrase it as "not found in our search", not "does not exist".

| Dataset | What it has | Useful for this thesis? |
|---|---|---|
| **PMo** (Brissard, Cuppens, Zouaq; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15857589, CC BY 4.0) | 55 descriptions + standardised BPMN, 9 representations | Current base. No legal steps in the references (fixed by the silver reference). |
| **PET** (Bellan et al., Hugging Face) | 45 descriptions annotated with activities, actors, gateways and relations | No BPMN files (they would have to be derived from the annotations) and no legal content. Not a better base. |
| **Kourani et al. knowledge-driven hallucination benchmark** (github.com/antonov1/process-hallucinations) | 4 processes (sales order, booking, complaints, audit), each with normal, reversed and shuffled descriptions that contradict typical domain knowledge | Very close to your question (does the model follow the description or its own knowledge?) but tiny. Possible small extra test, not a replacement. |
| **BREX / "Business as Rulesual"** (github.com/oYoungCo/Business-as-Rulesual) | 409 business documents, 2,855 expert-annotated condition→action rules and rule flows | Human-made rule conditions. Could check how well your applicability agent splits rules into conditions, but it has no process descriptions with BPMN. |
| **AI Act Evaluation Benchmark** (Davvetas et al., arXiv 2603.09435) | 339 AI-system scenarios with related articles and obligations | Scenario → which obligations apply is close to applicability, **but the labels were generated by an LLM and not expert-validated**, so it is not gold either. |
| Sai & Rinderle-Ma 2026 (regulatory texts → process models) | 5 use cases (incl. GDPR) with process models | No public dataset link found; you could email the authors. |
| Sekar et al. 2026 (medical guidelines → BPMN) | 3 Japanese municipal guidelines + human BPMN | Not stated as public; different domain. |

**Recommendation:**
1. Keep PMo (public and citable) plus the silver legal reference.
2. Ask the Ulm PhD students about their dataset. Data from your supervisors' group is the strongest option.
3. Optionally email Sai / Rinderle-Ma for their 5 regulatory use cases.
4. Decide on any new data **before** the real runs.
